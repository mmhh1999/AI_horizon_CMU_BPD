# Advisor Ideation, Thu 2026-09-24 (before the event)

**Participants:** Meltem Sahin Ozkoc, Minghao Xu, David Liu (team, with other lab members present); Azadeh Sawyer and Vivian Loftness (advisors).
**Source:** meeting transcript; a cleaned summary of the hackathon-related part only. Ideas and discussion only; no code (consistent with the rule that ideas may be brought, code may not).

## Takeaways

- **Don't build a generic real-estate ranker.** The brief reads as "compare duplex / townhome / apartment / ADU / detached and rank them." The advisors pushed for a much richer outcome set than maximizing a developer's return: *show what happens* if you build each type (energy, community, access), not just which one wins.
- **Let the user set the weights.** Put outcome metrics on the table and have the stakeholder weight them to 100, since developers and residents have very different priorities.
- **Triple bottom line.** Metrics should cover economy, environment, and people, not only the economic side.
- **Environmental rules first:**
  - No greenfield development while so much underused land exists.
  - No building on toxic sites.
  - Preserve forest and farmland.
- **Layer order for a county-scale view:**
  1. **Vacancy.** It indicates economic distress and shows there is no need to build on greenfields.
  2. **Preserve green space.**
  3. **Transit-oriented development.** Direct investment to transit nodes and walkable places, not to sites where every trip needs a car.
- **Don't treat all areas the same.** Larimer is not Shadyside or Downtown. But avoid steering people into pigeonholes ("only look in Mt. Lebanon").
- **Underserved areas.** If every new unit in an underserved neighborhood is low-income, the neighborhood keeps declining. Mix incomes.
- **Typologies worth adding:**
  - **ADUs.** An age-in-place idea and a second-income idea (rent or family care). Usually a homeowner's project, rarely a developer's.
  - **Senior housing as a network.** Ground-floor senior units spread through a multigenerational neighborhood, with care that moves between homes, rather than a tower of older residents.
  - **Co-housing.** Smaller private units plus shared amenities. Urban co-housing is more common in Europe. Collect urban case studies.
- **Knowledge base.** If AI is used for reasoning, ground it in a curated set of references rather than the open internet.
- **Solar and surface area.** Bring in solar exposure and surface-area measures, which play to the team's architecture background.
- **Access.** Walkability, transit, grocery, schools, and for families with small children, schools and other kids nearby.

## How this shows up in the product

- The weights-to-100 idea became the Stakeholder Priorities panel.
- The vacancy → green space → TOD order became the community-first flow and the Opportunity Layer (see [2026-09-26-advisor-review.md](2026-09-26-advisor-review.md)).
- Multigenerational housing, ADUs, and co-housing informed the expanded housing types.
